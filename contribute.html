Contributors

Write for AIToolGrade

We are building the most credible AI tools review site on the web. That means reviews built on verified pricing, official documentation, and documented features — not marketing copy dressed up as analysis. If you can research a tool carefully and check every claim against its official sources, we would like to hear from you.

What we are looking for

We cover AI tools across six categories. Right now we are actively looking for contributors in each of these areas:

AI Coding ToolsCursor, GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, Bolt.new — writers with a software development background
AI Image GenerationMidjourney, Adobe Firefly, Leonardo AI — writers who know design and visual workflows
AI Video ToolsRunway, Synthesia, HeyGen — writers who know video production
AI Writing ToolsJasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic — writers who know content marketing
Automation Toolsn8n, Zapier, Make — writers who understand ops and workflow automation
AI ProductivityNotion AI, Perplexity, ChatGPT — writers who understand knowledge work and research

We are looking for people who can read a pricing page closely, work through the documentation, and turn what they find into a clear call: what the tool does, what it really costs, and who it is right for.

The ideal contributor

  • Works from primary sources: official pricing pages, documentation, changelogs, and release notes
  • Checks every factual claim against an official source and notes where it came from
  • Keeps documented facts and community sentiment separate, and labels each clearly
  • Has a professional background relevant to the category they are covering
  • Can write clearly in English — we edit for format and style, not substance

What we offer contributors

✍️
Team credit

Contributions publish under the AIToolGrade Team byline, like every review on the site. We do not run personal bylines or author bios.

📐
Full editorial support

We handle structure, formatting, SEO optimisation, and publishing. You write the substance — we take care of everything else.

💰
Revenue sharing as the site grows

We are not paying for contributions right now, but as AIToolGrade monetises we intend to share that with contributors whose reviews drive traffic.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish paid placements or sponsored reviews
  • We do not accept reviews from people employed by or affiliated with the tool being reviewed
  • We do not publish reviews that read like marketing copy — honest assessments only
  • We do not add unsourced claims — if a point cannot be checked against an official source or clearly attributed, it does not go in

How it works

1

Reach out

Email us at rich@aitoolgrade.com with the tool you would like to cover and a sentence about your background in that category.

2

We send you the brief

If it is a good fit, we send you our review template and research checklist — around 800 to 1,200 words. Two weeks turnaround is fine.

3

You write, we edit

You submit a draft with a source for each factual claim. We check those claims against official pricing and documentation, then edit for format and style — we never change your opinions, scores, or verdict. You approve the final version before it goes live.

4

We publish under the team byline

Your review goes live under the AIToolGrade Team byline, the same as everything else on the site. We will let you know when it is published.

5

We keep it updated

When a major update ships, we reach back out to ask if anything needs updating. You are not obligated — but it keeps the review credible and current.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a professional writer?

No. We care about what you know, not how polished your prose is. We handle the editing.

Can I cover a tool my team uses at work?

Yes. Knowing the category helps you know where to look. The review itself still has to rest on official pricing, documentation, and attributed community sentiment, the same as every house review. The only restriction is that you cannot be employed by or financially affiliated with the company that makes the tool.

What if I have a mixed or negative opinion of the tool?

Good. That is exactly what we want. Honest assessments including real criticisms are what this site is built on.

Can I review a tool that already has a review on the site?

Yes — if your research goes significantly deeper than what is currently published, we are open to updating the existing review. It stays under the AIToolGrade Team byline.

Will you change my scores or verdict?

Never. Your scores and verdict are yours. We fact-check claims against official sources and edit structure and style, not substance.
